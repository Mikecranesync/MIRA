# FactoryLM is off base — UX recon report

**Date:** 2026-09-07
**Source research:** `~/Downloads/` recon bundle produced by Claude on 2026-09-07 —
`README.md`, `AI_APP_COMMON_PATTERN.md`, `FACTORYLM_UX_GRAMMAR.md`,
`FACTORYLM_UX_ACCEPTANCE.md`, `CURRENT_STATE_AUDIT.md`, and five per-product
`PRODUCT_ANATOMY.md` files (ChatGPT baseline; Claude / Gemini / Perplexity / Grok deltas).
**Surface audited:** `app.factorylm.com`, logged in as Mike Harper (Owner), dark theme, 2026-09-07.
**Method:** the same walkthrough framework applied to five mature AI products and then to FactoryLM.

This report exists to say one thing plainly: **the product a stranger actually touches is
built backwards from where we said we were, and it fails our own beta gate at the first step.**
It is a synthesis — the raw evidence and the full test suite are in the source files above.

---

## 1. The one-sentence finding

> **FactoryLM is built as an operations console that contains an AI feature.
> Every mature AI product is built as an AI surface that contains operations.**

That single inversion generates almost every specific defect below. It is not a theme, a
tone, or a coat of paint — it is the information architecture. Modern AI-app grammar
(home = greeting + focused text box; one persistent shell; one `+` for every capability;
user-message-bubble / assistant-document asymmetry; ≤3 levels deep; one accent colour)
**converged four products ago.** We spent our design budget re-deciding where the sidebar
lives instead of on the one thing that is actually ours to get right: the domain object.

Claude's domain object is a durable artifact. Perplexity's is a cited result. ChatGPT's is a
project of connected work. **FactoryLM's object is a machine that is currently broken and a
technician who has to fix it** — asset identity, evidence, provenance to a manual page, and a
closed loop to a work order. That is where every unit of effort should have gone. Instead the
effort went into KPI tiles, a proposal-flywheel progress bar, a 47-node knowledge graph, and a
dozen invented nouns.

---

## 2. Why this is "off base" against *our own* north star — not just against convention

This is the part that should sting, because it is measured against what **we** wrote, not
against what ChatGPT does.

Our `CLAUDE.md` states the release gate for the entire "Path to Beta Testers" phase:

> **🚦 BETA GATE — A stranger can upload their own equipment manual and get a cited answer
> without Mike manually fixing anything.** … **Status (2026-06-17): MET / PASSING on deploy truth.**

The recon independently, and without knowing that sentence, wrote the single test it says
outranks all others:

> **Z-1 · The five-minute stranger test.** Hand a phone running FactoryLM to a maintenance
> technician who has never seen it, who uses ChatGPT weekly. Say only: *"Find out what the
> grinding noise on that conveyor might be."* Then say nothing for five minutes.
> **Pass:** they start typing or scanning without asking a question; they reach a **cited answer**
> within five minutes without instruction; they can say afterwards where the answer came from.

**These are the same test.** Our beta gate *is* Z-1 with an upload step in front of it.

And Z-1 fails at step one, on the live product, today:

- **There is no composer on the home screen.** The Command Board opens on KPI tiles, an
  `L5 — Proposal flywheel` progress bar, and a work-order feed. A technician cannot ask anything
  from the landing screen. (`CURRENT_STATE_AUDIT` S1-01 / FLM-A-01.)
- **When a message *is* sent, it returns `Chat unavailable (412). Try again or refresh the page.`**
  — a raw HTTP status code, in a static non-dismissible red banner permanently occupying a slot
  in the transcript, with no retry button. Generation never succeeded during the walk, so citation
  behaviour could not even be observed. (S1-05 / FLM-I-01.)

So: **the beta gate is green in CI and red in reality.** `tests/beta/beta_ready_upload_retrieval_citation.py`
proves the *backend* upload→retrieval→citation path returns rows. It says nothing about whether a
human being can reach that answer through the interface — and the recon shows they cannot. We have
been measuring the pipe and reporting it as the product. "MET / PASSING on deploy truth" is true of
the retrieval path and false of the thing a stranger holds in their hand.

**This is the core of "way off base": the gap between our declared status and the observed
surface is not a bug backlog, it is a category error about what we were supposed to be building.**

## 3. The seven universal-convention breaks

Each of these breaks a pattern that is present in 5/5 (or every observable) reference product.
A modern-app user reads each one as a bug, not a preference. Full detail in `CURRENT_STATE_AUDIT.md`.

| # | Violation | What the user experiences |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **No composer on the home screen.** Command Board opens on KPI tiles, a flywheel bar, a WO feed. | Cannot ask anything from the landing screen. Reaching the AI takes three navigations. |
| 2 | **`Scan` leaves the app shell entirely** — sidebar gone, theme flips dark→light, no in-app back. | Trapped on the product's most important screen (photo→nameplate is the spine). |
| 3 | **Message alignment does not encode speaker.** A long blue message renders left-aligned while other user messages render right-aligned in the same blue. | Cannot tell who said what. |
| 4 | **Raw markdown leaks into output** — the MIRA greeting shows literal `**Garage Conveyor**`. | The first message a user ever sees looks broken. |
| 5 | **Errors show HTTP status codes with no recovery** — `Chat unavailable (412)`, static red banner, no retry. | `412` means nothing; "refresh the page" is the developer's job handed to the user. |
| 6 | **A failed message exists twice** — as a sent bubble *and* still in the composer. | Cannot tell whether it sent. |
| 7 | **No message actions anywhere.** No copy, retry, feedback, sources, or timestamps. | A technician cannot paste a diagnosis into a work order — the closed loop the product exists for. |

## 4. The structural findings underneath them

Beyond the seven, the audit records a consistent pattern of **showing the system's internals as
the user's workspace**:

- **13 primary nav items** against a category ceiling of 5–6 (ChatGPT 5, Claude 5, Gemini 6).
- **8 tabs on an asset page** (`Details · Ask MIRA · Activity · Work Orders · Documents · Parts ·
  Intel · Validate`) against ChatGPT's project page's 2.
- **~a dozen invented nouns** — `Namespace`, `Command Board`, `Command Center`, `Proposal flywheel`,
  `Verified edges`, `Intel`, `Validate`, `Channels` — two of which (`Command Board` / `Command
  Center`) differ by one word. Perplexity was marked down *in this study* for inventing **one**
  (`Sessions`).
- **`Knowledge` opens on a 47-node force-directed graph** with type checkboxes and a
  verified/proposed edge legend — not on the manuals it is supposed to hold.
- **The home screen's top element is a readiness bar toward the *system's* state**
  (`L5 — Proposal flywheel · once verified outnumber proposed you cross L6`), not the user's work.
- **`MODEL: 1` and `SERIAL: 1` presented as data.** A nameplate-extraction product displaying
  `Model: 1` is displaying that its own spine has not run — and dressing that failure as a fact.
- **Two assets both named `Stardust Racers`**, distinguished only by muted EQ numbers.
- **Raw UNS path as a primary label** — `enterprise.home_garage.conveyor_lab.conveyor_1` shown as
  an object's subtitle.
- **A full-width purple→blue gradient CTA** (`Chat with MIRA about this asset`) — the largest
  element on the asset page, styled exactly like the ad units users have trained themselves to skip.
- **No photo attach anywhere in the conversation composer** — in a product whose entire spine is
  photo → nameplate → manual → work order.
- **A leaked internal string** (`monday context loading…`) in the Scan page header.

No reference product shows its own data structure as a primary surface. We show ours on the home
screen, the knowledge page, and the command center.

## 5. What is already right — protect these, do not "redesign" them

The recon was deliberate about recording strengths, because the fix is presentation and
navigation, **not** the data model:

- The **`Asset-scoped` badge** on the MIRA chat — conceptually equal to ChatGPT's scoped placeholder.
- The **greeting names the asset and its ID** (`Ask me anything about Garage Conveyor (OTHE-3PMTTSX8)`).
- The **Assets index** (H1 + search + status chips + card grid) — the most conventional screen in
  the product.
- **`Scan plate` / `Upload photo`** as the two entry paths — the right two, correctly ranked.
- The **underlying object model** — assets, work orders, documents, parts, activity, scans are the
  correct nouns for this domain.

> **The problem is presentation and navigation, not the data model.** That is the good news: the
> expensive, hard-won part (the maintenance-context layer, the object graph) is sound. The cheap
> part (the shell that wraps it) is what's wrong — and the shell grammar is already solved by the
> whole industry, so there is nothing to invent.

---

## 6. The five changes that unblock everything else

From `README.md` §4 and the grammar. Ordered by leverage:

1. **Make the composer the home screen** — greeting, focused input, three suggestions drawn from
   live equipment state. The KPI tiles and flywheel bar leave.
2. **Never break the shell** — no route removes the sidebar, changes the theme, or leaves the user
   without an in-app back. (Kills the `Scan` trap.)
3. **Fix the conversation** — render markdown; enforce user-right-bubble / assistant-left-no-bubble;
   make suggestions tappable chips; put a persistent action row under every answer with **copy**
   as non-negotiable.
4. **Rewrite errors to the ChatGPT rule** — degrade to the nearest working state, explain in plain
   language, offer a **button**. No status codes, no permanent red banners.
   (`Couldn't reach MIRA. Your message is saved.` `[Retry]` `[×]`.)
5. **Make the asset page ChatGPT's project page** — scoped composer on top, four tabs below
   (`Chat · Details · History · Files`). The gradient banner and the `Ask MIRA` tab both disappear,
   because asking stops being a destination.

Then the mechanical reductions: 13 nav items → 5 + `More ›`; 8 tabs → 4; every invented noun → a
word a technician already uses (`Command Board`→`Home`, `Knowledge`→`Manuals`, `Namespace`→
`Equipment map`, …).

## 7. Where we should *intentionally* differ (this is the moat, and it aligns with the north star)

The recon is explicit that most of the app is convention we should just follow — but it names
**~14–19 deliberate departures** that maintenance work genuinely requires. The six that matter,
and note how tightly they map onto `NORTH_STAR.md` and the beta gate:

1. **Inline citation chips naming the manual and page** — `📖 SKF 6205 · p.14`, not `[3]`. *This is
   the product's whole commercial claim* — "diagnose with cited sources." A number tells a
   technician nothing until they click; a name lets them judge the claim inside the sentence.
2. **Unsourced claims are visibly labelled** — `⚠ General guidance — no manual on file for this
   asset`. A wrong torque spec has physical consequences; "the manual says" vs "generally speaking"
   must be visible in the answer, not inferred from the absence of a chip.
3. **Offline is a first-class state, not an error** — cached manuals readable, composer usable,
   messages queue and send on reconnect. **No reference product offers a pattern here** — our users
   are in steel buildings next to VFDs, not at desks. Genuine, defensible differentiation.
4. **The camera is promoted out of the `+` menu** into its own permanent composer button — because
   photo→nameplate is our spine (ChatGPT promotes exactly one capability: voice; we promote ours).
5. **The waiting state names the step** — `Reading the nameplate…` → `Finding the manual…` →
   `Checking work order history…` — turning a variable multi-step pipeline from "broken" into
   "diagnostic."
6. **A permanent, tappable scope badge** naming the machine — because answering about the wrong
   machine is a safety problem, not an inconvenience.

Every one of these is downstream of the same object (a broken machine + a technician). None of them
is where our current design energy is going.

## 8. The acceptance model already exists — adopt it as the gate

`FACTORYLM_UX_ACCEPTANCE.md` is **74 outside-in tests** (28 P0 ship gates, 38 P1 demo gates,
8 P2), every one judged from what is on screen — none asserts an endpoint returned 200 or a
component mounted. That is exactly the discipline we were missing: our beta gate asserts the
backend, this suite asserts the product. The P0 failures observed live today cluster in five areas —
composer-is-home, speaker identity + rendering, shell never broken, answers actionable +
attributable, errors humane + non-destructive — four of which have **observed** current-state
failures and the fifth (citations) is untested only because generation never succeeded.

**Recommendation:** treat Z-1 and the 28 P0 tests as a required, human-observable gate that sits
*in front of* the CI retrieval gate — not instead of it. Green retrieval + failed Z-1 is exactly
the false "MET / PASSING" we shipped.

## 9. Honest limits of the research (so we weight it correctly)

The recon states its own limits plainly, and we should carry them forward rather than overclaim:

- **Grok and Perplexity were walked logged out** — their authenticated shell, history and chat
  lifecycle are untested, so no shell/history/answer-lifecycle rule reaches `n=5/5`.
- **Mobile was excluded by scope** — all mobile guidance (grammar §8, tests section H) is inference
  from responsive structure and is labelled unvalidated. For a phone-first product this is the
  biggest untested area and should be the next recon.
- **Failure behaviour was tested in depth only in ChatGPT**, and incidentally in FactoryLM via a
  real 412; network loss and mid-stream generation failure were tested nowhere.
- **Citation and streaming behaviour in FactoryLM was never observed** — generation never succeeded
  during the walk. E-1/E-2 (the citation gates — our whole claim) are **untested, not passed**.
  Re-run the moment chat works.
- Some adopted rules rest on one or two products (message asymmetry, capped measure, retained
  reasoning) and are adopted on quality + domain fit, not consensus.
- Screenshots were captured live but returned no retrievable paths, so every evidence entry is a
  **reproducible journey** (route → action → resulting state) the team can re-run directly — which
  is more useful for verification than an opaque PNG.

---

## 10. The bottom line

We did the hard part — the maintenance-context layer and the object model are right. Then we
wrapped it in the one thing we were free *not* to invent (the AI-app shell) and inverted it,
turning an AI surface into an operations console. The result fails our own beta gate's spirit at
step one: a stranger cannot reach a cited answer, because there is no composer to type into and the
first send returns `412`.

**Nothing about fixing this requires research into where the sidebar goes. That is settled. The
work is to stop showing the system to the user, put the composer on the home screen, keep the
shell intact, make answers copyable and cited, and make errors humane — then spend the *entire*
remaining design budget on the domain object, which is the only place we can actually win.**
