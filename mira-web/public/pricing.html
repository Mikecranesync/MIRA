Questions

Is there a monthly price on the site during the five pilots?
No self-serve monthly menu. The public offer is the free early-adopter 30-day pilot for the first five approved teams (five seats each). Continuation beyond credited seats is custom with Mike.
What about the old Assessment / paid plan menu?
Retired from this public page while the early-adopter offer runs. Talk to Mike if you need a scoped engagement outside the five free pilots.
What is the early-adopter lifetime credit?
Early adopters among the first five awarded teams can lock in limited free lifetime credit covering those initial five seats. Transferability, cancel effects, and any extras are agreed with Mike — we do not invent amounts or fine print on this page.
Do you replace my CMMS?
No. MIRA stands alone on your manuals. CMMS connectors are optional add-ons — never implied as required for a pilot.
Why not a self-serve sign-up?
Pilots are approval-gated so we can confirm fit, manuals, and seat caps (five teams × five seats). Applying or emailing Mike is not the same as being awarded a team.
Multi-plant or more than five seats?
Talk to Mike. Extra seats beyond the credited five, and multi-plant scope, are custom — not self-serve checkout.
