UNS, in plain language

A shared structure
for your plant’s information.

UNS means Unified Namespace. It gives equipment and its information a consistent name and a place in the plant: site, area, line, machine and component.

A reading or document becomes more useful when you know which machine it belongs to. In a connected plant, live data uses that same context. FactoryLM’s focus is building the maintenance information inside that structure, including alongside an existing UNS.

Evidence lets you check.

A few things to know

Where MIRA fits.

Do I need a complete UNS to start?

No. Start with one equipment notebook, a manual or electrical print set and one question. Your broader namespace can be planned around the machines and information you already have.

Will every conversation automatically train MIRA?

Saving a conversation or note does not make it approved knowledge. The namespace-building approach keeps proposed relationships separate from human-reviewed facts.

Does MIRA replace my CMMS or control system?

MIRA starts with maintenance context and document answers. Your CMMS, SCADA and controls belong in an integration plan with their own permissions and availability checks. The self-service starting point requires no PLC connection.
