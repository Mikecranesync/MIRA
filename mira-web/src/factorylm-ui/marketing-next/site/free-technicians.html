Free technician access

Your personal MIRA workspace.

Free for individual technicians, with no trial deadline or credit card.

What the free offer means

A new verified technician account includes one personal workspace for one person. Free accounts do not expire at the end of a trial, require a credit card or enroll you in a paid plan.

The sign-up page shows whether new free accounts are currently open. This policy remains available for accounts already covered by the offer. Every technician can use their own personal account. Team invitations, shared crew workspaces, plant pilots and separately ordered services are arranged with FactoryLM. Discuss your plant rollout.

Verify your email

Account creation takes place in MIRA. Open the setup link sent to your work email, then confirm the account agreement. The website collects no account credentials.

Terms and privacy

This page describes the free personal-account offer. The FactoryLM Terms of Service and Privacy Policy also apply. Paid assessments and separately ordered plant or team services retain their own agreed scope.

Existing accounts retain their current access terms. Product capabilities and limits are described on the limitations page.
