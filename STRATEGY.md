# FactoryLM Go-To-Market Strategy

**Current direction:** September 5, 2026 — [NORTH_STAR.md](NORTH_STAR.md).
**Delivery:** [Unified plan](docs/product/2026-09-05-sellable-app-alignment.md), [MIRA #3586](https://github.com/Mikecranesync/MIRA/issues/3586).

## What we sell

The existing FactoryLM app becomes a familiar, clean place to talk to MIRA, use equipment/document evidence, and keep useful maintenance work. A technician starts with a question, adds context as needed, inspects a cited answer, and returns to the saved result.

The interface makes starting easy. Trusted help using the customer's actual material and retained work makes it valuable. Drive intelligence, ingestion, namespaces, and integrations support this promise; they do not require separate product pitches for the first release.

## First customer proof

Start with a bounded maintenance pilot: one customer, one repeatable job, and representative equipment/documents. Confirm the technician who uses it, the buyer who approves it, current workflow, success criteria, and support expectations. The existing maintenance-manager/technician segment is a starting hypothesis; select a specific pilot from real customer evidence.

Before selling access, prove fresh-user onboarding, photo/manual ingestion, correctly scoped cited answers or useful refusal, follow-up continuity, saved work, and reopening on a supported device. Verify the existing access/payment path, privacy boundaries, cost, support, and recovery. Follow release approvals.

## Decisions and measures

- Approve the pilot offer, price, limits, support scope, and purchase/access path using customer and cost evidence.
- Track activation, repeat use, useful grounded outcomes, unresolved jobs, support effort, and cost per outcome against a named build and customer scope.
- Choose numerical goals after measuring the baseline. Do not publish historical market, savings, pricing, or revenue assumptions as verified facts.
- Put product gaps and customer learning into the unified tracker. Infrastructure work receives priority through an app blocker, useful capability, or existing maintenance obligation.
