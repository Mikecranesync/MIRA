## What the gate run taught us (and fixed)

1. **A judge outage produces a green report.** Groq 400'd every case; `judge_baseline` recorded them as
   `unscored`; `report.py` saw `0 dangerous / 0 cases` and printed RELEASE CANDIDATE. The judge is now
   provider-configurable (`JUDGE_API_KEY`/`JUDGE_API_URL`/`JUDGE_MODEL` → `OPENAI_API_KEY` → legacy Groq) and
   records `judge_url` / `judge_model` in `_summary.json`. **Still open:** `report.py` should treat
   `scored == 0` as INFRA_FAILURE, not a pass — a one-line follow-up.
2. **The gate labelled the wrong revision.** `--sha` was not passed through to `run_technician`; the manifest
   recorded the checkout's HEAD. Fixed.
3. **Grounded correctness is a function of the eval notebook.** The notebook holds two VFD manuals; eleven
   `must_cite` cases are about other equipment and abstained. Either scope cases to the corpus or give the
   notebook the manuals the cases cite.
