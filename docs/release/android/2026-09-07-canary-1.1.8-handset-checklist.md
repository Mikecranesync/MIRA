## The one observation that matters

Everything else is secondary. This is the bug you reported:

> **Open the app → About & Updates → Check now.**
>
> It must say **Update ready** (because 1.1.8 > 1.1.7).
> Tap **Restart**. After it comes back, About must show **Active bundle `1.1.8-a36c63bf`**.
>
> Then tap **Check now** a second time.
> **It must now say "Up to date" and must NOT download anything.**

That second tap is the fix. Before this change, with the canary offering the bundle you
were already running, the app re-downloaded it every time and then reported a confusing
integrity failure. If tap two says anything other than "Up to date" — especially if it
downloads — **that is the defect not fixed, and the most useful thing you can tell me.**

⚠️ Treat "Active bundle" as *fetched*, not *applied*, until you have restarted and seen
the new id. That distinction is what made #3637 invisible for weeks.
