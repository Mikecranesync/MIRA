## A useful collaboration request—not an outreach instruction

Start with a narrow mutual problem: **how should independent technicians judge whether an assistant improved the next maintenance decision?** Offer a proposed case schema, evidence-request interface, and scoring rubric for critique. Ask which assumptions fail in practice and what would make a small recorded-case pilot credible. Do not lead with an unsupported reliability claim or a request for endorsements.

Possible contributions should remain distinct: evaluation-method feedback, independent adjudication, authorized synthetic/recorded cases, human-factors study advice, or introductions made only with permission. Any study, data exchange, compensation, or outreach needs its own agreement. Research interest is not a sales lead, and a public profile is not consent to contact through private channels.
