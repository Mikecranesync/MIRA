# FactoryLM marketing foundation

Working research for FactoryLM's market entry, written **Oct 10, 2026**. These
are drafts for Mike Harper (CEO), not approved go-to-market doctrine.

**Decisions still pending Mike's approval:** the $399/mo per-site price, and the
postal address required in the cold email (CAN-SPAM). Do not publish, send, or
treat either as locked until he signs off.

**Claim rule:** marketing copy may only cite proven MIRA behavior — uploaded
OEM manuals, page-level citations, and honest refusal when the manual does not
cover the question. Do not claim PLC-tag mapping, CMMS write-back, fault-history
answers, live nameplate discovery, or any other capability that is not in the
proven inventory.
