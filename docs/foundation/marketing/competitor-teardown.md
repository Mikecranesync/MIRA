**The pattern:** the big CMMS vendors put "ask your manuals" behind their top tier or an extra license. The enterprise platforms (Siemens, Augmentir, Dozuki, Aquant) sell a whole platform and run long sales cycles. Five startups (Quintess, Satori, YAFEX, Rheba, Machine Pilot) pitch nearly our exact promise, but **none of them is a US-based offer for small plants with a published, flat site price.** Only Machine Pilot publishes a price, and it's per machine and much higher.

- **Buyer and size:** Maintenance and operations leaders, single site to global. "Over 14,000 companies rely on MaintainX." https://www.getmaintainx.com/
- **Pricing:** Basic $0; Essential $20/user/mo annual ($25 monthly); Premium $65/user/mo annual ($75 monthly); Enterprise custom. The pricing page lists "MaintainX Assist" under Enterprise only. The help center's availability tables mark the manual features as Enterprise only. https://www.getmaintainx.com/pricing ; https://help.getmaintainx.com/manage-asset-manuals-with-maintainx-copilot
- **Headline:** "Intelligent maintenance and asset management"
- **Subhead:** "Increase equipment uptime, reduce costs, and boost frontline productivity with the leading AI-powered CMMS and enterprise asset management platform."
- **Claims and proof (theirs):** "200 sites implemented in 9 weeks", "70% less mean time to repair", "53% lower maintenance costs", "40+ plants standardized maintenance". Named case studies: Cintas, Michael's Redimix, US LBM, Interroll. SOC 2 Type 2, ISO 27001, SAP Silver Partner. - **Channels:** blog, webinars, case studies, academy, community, podcast ("The Wrench Factor"), ROI calculator, comparison pages (vs Limble, UpKeep, Maximo, Fiix, eMaint, Tractian), YouTube, LinkedIn, X, free plan. https://www.getmaintainx.com/
