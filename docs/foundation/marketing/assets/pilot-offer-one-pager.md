# FactoryLM 30-Day Pilot
### Your line's manuals, answering your crew, with the page on every answer.

**Price:** $500 for 30 days. If you continue, it's **$399/month per site** (up to 15 users), and the $500 is credited to your first months. Cancel anytime after the pilot; no long contract.

---

## What you get
- **We load the manuals.** Send us (or let us scan) the OEM manuals for **up to 25 machines on one production line**, any brand. We load them for you, so your crew doesn't do data entry.
- **Your crew asks in plain English.** From the web at app.factorylm.com (and the FactoryLM app once it's available for your crew's phones).
- **Every answer from your manuals shows the manual and page.** Your tech can open the page and check it before acting.
- **It says when your manuals don't cover it.** Answers that aren't from your manuals are clearly labeled as general.
- **A direct line to the founder.** Mike Harper sets up the pilot with you, on site in Central Florida or remote anywhere.

## What we need from you
1. A list of the machines on the line, and the manuals you have (PDF, or paper we can scan).
2. One person as the pilot lead (usually the maintenance manager or lead tech).
3. 15 minutes at kickoff and at the end of each week.

## How we measure it (together)
| Measure | Target to call it a success |
|---|---|
| Crew using it | At least half the crew asks a question each week |
| Right page | At least 80% of answers drawn from your manuals cite the right page (we check them with you) |
| Your call | You'd be annoyed if we took it away |

You get a short written summary at day 30: questions asked, which machines, cited-page accuracy, and gaps (missing manuals).

## What it does *not* do (yet)
- It doesn't replace your CMMS or create work orders. It works beside whatever you use.
- It doesn't connect to PLCs or read live machine data.
- It doesn't use your work-order history; it answers from the manuals you give it.
- It doesn't give step-by-step answers for lockout, arc flash or confined-space work. Follow your site's procedures.
- It's not SOC 2 audited yet. Your data is kept separate from other customers' and isn't used to train outside AI models. See factorylm.com/security.

## Try it before you buy
Send one manual and three questions your techs actually ask. We'll send back cited answers within one business day, free.
