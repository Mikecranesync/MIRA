## 2. Copy variants (2–3)

CTA button (Meta): **Learn more** for website traffic, or **Get quote** / **Contact us** if using Instant Form. Never "Shop now" or "Buy".

Recommended primary for first test: **Variant A**.

### Variant A — "Ask the manual" (recommended)
**Primary text:**
> When a machine is down, the answer is usually already in the OEM manual — if you can find the page.
>
> MIRA is an equipment notebook for your maintenance crew. Attach the manuals for one machine. Ask a question. When the manual covers it, MIRA answers and cites the page. When it doesn't, it says so — no guess, no fake citation.
>
> Built for plants that want answers from *their* manuals. No CMMS required. Doesn't connect to your PLCs.
>
> Mike Harper works with crews onsite in Central Florida or remotely. Talk to Mike — drafts only, no pressure.

**Headline:** Ask the manual. See the page.
**Description:** Answers cite the OEM page — or say the manual doesn't cover it.
**CTA:** Learn more

### Variant B — small-crew / no-CMMS
**Primary text:**
> Small maintenance crew. Big binder of manuals. No time to dig.
>
> FactoryLM's MIRA gives each machine its own notebook with its manuals attached. Ask from the browser (desktop citation opens the page). There's also an Android app for the same conversation.
>
> It isn't a CMMS and doesn't replace one. You don't need a CMMS to use it. It doesn't read PLC data.
>
> Central Florida plants: talk to Mike about trying it on one machine.

**Headline:** One notebook per machine
**Description:** Ask your manuals. See the cited page. No CMMS needed.
**CTA:** Contact us

### Variant C — local founder
**Primary text:**
> I'm Mike Harper. I work with maintenance crews in Central Florida — or remotely — to put the right OEM manuals on one machine and make them searchable with page citations.
>
> MIRA answers from the manuals you attach. If the manual doesn't cover the question, it says so instead of guessing.
>
> Reference tool only. Follow your site procedure and the OEM manual for lockout and any hands-on work.
>
> Want a look at one machine? Email me.

**Headline:** Central Florida. One machine. Your manuals.
**Description:** Talk to Mike about an equipment notebook for your crew.
**CTA:** Contact us

### Lines deliberately left out of all variants
- Any dollar amount or "pilot fee"
- Competitor names
- "Fixes fault codes" / general fault-code promise (general page-finding is PARTIAL; only a narrow two-manual test is PROVEN — too narrow for ad copy)
- Accuracy percentages, customer counts, time-saved claims
- "Works on your phone" for opening citations (phone citation viewer still broken)
- CMMS sync, work orders, PLC tags, predictive anything

## 5. Test plan

**Budget:** Mike to set. (Do not invent a number.)

**Structure:** 1 campaign → 1 ad set (Central Florida audience above) → 3 ads (Variants A/B/C) sharing the square creative first. Landscape as a second creative only after A wins on CTR.

**Duration:** until each variant has enough impressions for a read, or Mike stops — no calendar promise in public copy.

**Success signals (internal):**
- CTR above the other variants
- Cost per Instant Form lead / email that Mike actually replies to
- At least one discovery call booked from paid (qualitative win)

**Kill / pause rules:**
- Any comment asking for prices → reply privately "Talk to Mike: mike@factorylm.com", never post a number
- Any safety / fault-reset question in comments → "Follow your site procedure and the OEM manual. MIRA is a reference tool."
- Creative or copy that drifts into PARTIAL claims → pause and fix

**What this test is not:** a customer-acquisition blast. It's one controlled creative + message test to refine before any larger spend. Outreach/cold email remain on hold.
