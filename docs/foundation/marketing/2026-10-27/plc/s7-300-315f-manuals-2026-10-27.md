## Edition traps
- **CPU scope:** the two 31x manuals list standard CPUs only (e.g. 315-2 DP 6ES7315-2AH14 FW V3.3; 315-2 PN/DP 6ES7315-2EH14 FW V3.2). **No 315F-2 is listed.** For the F-CPU the procedures are analogous; the F-specific delta is in the Product Information (T-064: PN/DP PI FETCHED; DP PI BLOCKED — browser/SIOS only). Do not cite 31x pages as if they named the 315F.
- **Firmware:** 03/2011 editions target FW V3.2/V3.3; older CPUs (e.g. -2FJ13/-2FH13, FW V2.x) may differ. The OP's "6ES7-325-2FJ14" is a typo; **6ES7315-2FJ14-0AB0 = CPU 315F-2 PN/DP** (confirmed T-064 from Siemens Product Information entry 38993109 and Siemens type-approval PDF on cache).
- **315F-2 DP vs PN/DP:** Distributed Safety notes some functions only for 315F-2 PN/DP "via PN interface" (pp.42, 133, 173, 249); MMC handling text mentions 315F-2 PN/DP explicitly (p.286).
- **Engineering tool:** all three are **STEP 7 classic (V5.x)** + Distributed Safety V5.4 SP4. TIA Portal projects use STEP 7 Safety (docs.tia.siemens.cloud, browser-only, not fetched); menus/terms differ. A safety program compiled in V5.4 SP4 has version-change rules (pp.19–21: collective signature changes SP3→SP4; downgrade requires uninstall).
- Older editions may sit under the same entry IDs with other att_ numbers; these are the att_ given in the archaeology brief.
