The ONLY on board RJ-45 port on an ACS355 is RS-232 and the connections to it are in the first post of this thread.
For anything else you need to add an option module and each has a document detailing its wiring. What is the part number of your option module?

Or do you need assistance with deduction?
1 - Transmit
3 - Receive
2 & 6 - Ground
The others are not used.

For A5001 (Drive not responding) issue, it was found that there is no DC3.3V at the connector X3 at WINT1011C power board.

IC A843 (DC DC buck converter P/N: ADP2301AUJZ) was found faulty.

After replacement, no more A5001.
