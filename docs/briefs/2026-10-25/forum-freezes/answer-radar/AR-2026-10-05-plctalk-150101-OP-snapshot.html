Hello everyone,

I am working with a Schneider Electric TM172PDG28R PLC and a TM171EP14R expansion module. I am trying to establish communication between the PLC and the expansion module over CAN, but the communication is not being established.

Hardware:​

  • PLC: TM172PDG28R
  • Expansion Module: TM171EP14R
  • Communication: CAN

Current Settings:​

  • I have assigned CAN ID = 2 to the expansion module.
  • PLC parameter Addr_CAN_OB = 1.
  • The expansion module is physically located very close to the PLC.
  • CAN communication is still not established.
I have checked the basic configuration, but I am still unable to establish communication.

My questions:​

  1. Is Addr_CAN_OB = 1 correct for the TM171EP14R?
  2. What should be the correct CAN Node ID/address for the TM171EP14R?
  3. What CAN baud rate should be configured for the TM172PDG28R and TM171EP14R?
  4. Are there any specific DIP-switch settings required on the TM171EP14R for CAN addressing?
  5. Is 120 Ω termination required between the PLC and the expansion module when there are only two CAN nodes?
  6. Is there any additional configuration or initialization required in the TM172PbG42R program for the TM171EP14R to be recognized?
I would appreciate it if someone could guide me through the correct CAN configuration and troubleshooting steps.

Thank you.
