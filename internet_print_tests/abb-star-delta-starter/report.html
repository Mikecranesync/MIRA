# YKA ..-30 / YDA ..-30 Star-Delta Starter — Open Type, Thermal O/L Protection

This is sheet **2/4** of ABB document **1SBC 0095 00 R1001**. It shows three diagrams for an open-type star-delta starter with thermal overload protection.

## 1. Power Circuit (top diagram)

Three contactors wired to a 3-phase motor:

- **KM1 — Line (Main) contactor**: connects incoming lines **1/L1, 3/L2, 5/L3** to motor terminals **2/T1, 4/T2, 6/T3**. Auxiliary contacts shown: **13-14 (NO)** and **21-22 (NC)**.
- **KM2 — Delta contactor**: same line/motor terminal pattern. Auxiliary contacts **13-14 (NO)** and **21-22 (NC)**.
- **KM3 — Star contactor**: same line/motor terminal pattern. Auxiliary contacts **13-14 (NO)** and **21-22 (NC)**.
- **KT — Star-Delta timer**: terminals **16, 18, 15, A1, A2**; timed contacts **t1** (instantaneous) and **t1+t2** (delayed).
- **FR1 — Thermal Overload Relay**: heater terminals **2/T1, 4/T2, 6/T3**; control terminals **95-96 (NC)** and **97-98 (NO)**.
- Two motor windings shown with terminals **U1/V1/W1** and **U2/V2/W2**.

## 2. Local Control (bottom left)

Control supply **Us / N** taken from **KM3 : 3/L2**.

Control string (top to bottom):
- **O/R** (Off/Reset pushbutton) in series with **FR1 95-96** (O/L trip).
- **I** (Start pushbutton) in parallel with **KM1 23-24** (holding contact).
- **KT** timer coil fed through **KM1 23-24**, with timed contact **t1+t2** switching between **KM3** (star) and **KM2** (delta) coils.
- **KM3**, **KM2**, **KM1** coils each with their own **13-14 NO** interlock to prevent KM3 and KM2 from being energized together.

## 3. Remote Control (bottom right)

Same control logic as Local, but with an additional remote **O** (stop) and **I** (start) contact pair wired in series/parallel with the local pushbuttons — allowing remote start/stop while retaining local control.

## Operating Sequence (as drawn)

1. Press **I** → **KM1** energizes and seals via **KM1 23-24**.
2. **KM1** closes the line contactor; **KT** timer begins timing.
3. **KM3** (star) pulls in through **KT t1** contact, shorting motor windings in star.
4. After preset time, **KT t1+t2** transfers: **KM3** drops out, **KM2** (delta) pulls in — motor runs in delta.
5. Press **O/R** or an **FR1** trip opens the seal-in circuit and stops the motor.

---

**Note:** The print shows the *designed* wiring and contact states only. To confirm whether any contactor is actually energized or any contact is open/closed on the running machine, the technician must verify with a meter at the terminals.
