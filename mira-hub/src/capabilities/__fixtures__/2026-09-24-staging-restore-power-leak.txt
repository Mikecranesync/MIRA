The first thing to verify is that the panel is fully de‑energized and locked out before you do any work on the bus bars.  

1. **Lockout/Tagout** – Isolate the 480 V feeder, apply a lock and tag per your site LOTO procedure, and verify zero voltage with a properly rated, isolated tester.  
2. **Confirm isolation** – Check that all upstream breakers and disconnects are open and that the panel’s main breaker is OFF.  
3. **Notify** – Ensure all personnel are aware that the panel will be re‑energized for a short test and that the area is clear.  
4. **Re‑energize for measurement** – With the panel still locked out (the lock remains in place, but the breaker is closed), close the feeder breaker to apply voltage.  
5. **Clamp each phase** – Using a calibrated clamp‑on ammeter, safely clamp one phase at a time, recording the current. Keep the meter’s leads clear of any moving parts and maintain proper PPE.  
6. **De‑energize again** – After the measurements, open the feeder breaker, verify voltage is gone, then remove the lock/tag and restore normal operation.  

**Question:** Is the panel equipped with a dedicated test breaker or a removable fuse that allows a short‑duration energization without affecting the rest of the system? This will determine whether you can isolate the test to just this panel.
